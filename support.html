Frequently Asked Questions

Is an electronic certificate legally equivalent to a physical certificate?

Yes. Under Section 4 & 5 of the Information Technology Act 2000, digitally signed e-Certificates generated via CivicSeva / e-District portals possess identical legal validity in all courts, universities, and government recruitment processes.

How can I verify the authenticity of an issued certificate?

Every certificate contains a 2D Secure QR Code. Scanning the QR code with any smartphone camera instantly connects to the National e-District verification server displaying the official issuing officer's signature and timestamp.

What should I do if the SLA deadline for my application has passed?

If your application exceeds the statutory Service Level Agreement (7–15 days depending on the service), you can lodge an automated escalation directly using the "Raise Delayed Inquiry" tool on the status tracker or the CPGRAMS form on this page.

Can I access my certificates directly via DigiLocker?

Yes. Once approved by the Tehsildar or SDM, all issued documents automatically push to your DigiLocker repository under the "Issued Documents" tab mapped to your Aadhaar number.
